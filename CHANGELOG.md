# Changelog / 更新记录

## v1.0.0 — 2026-11-18

### English

Initial release of `partb-vrp-writing`, a writing skill for vehicle routing and related transportation operations research manuscripts intended for *Transportation Research Part B: Methodological*.

- Provides a **locate → assess → revise → recheck** workflow for planning, drafting, revision, and review, with guidance on argumentation, literature reviews, notation, formulations, algorithms, proofs, experiments, language, figures, and tables.
- Includes English instructions, six topic and evidence reference files, Codex display metadata, and a blank manuscript context template. The [English README](README.md) and [简体中文 README](README.zh-CN.md) link to each other; manuscript work follows the user's requested language.
- Separates academic requirements, adaptable writing practices, and author preferences. Preserves research assumptions and supported contributions, respects wording-only revision requests, and checks affected claims, notation, references, and bilingual passages.
- Records selected checked locations from Su et al. (2023), Sun et al. (2018), and Zamal et al. (2025). The record distinguishes author manuscripts from published versions and image inspection from text-only checks; Sun et al. supports text observations only. It does not claim a complete paper review or a journal-wide style survey.
- Retains the author's preference for **brighter figure colors**, with configurable black labels on white backgrounds and information-driven use of colors, shapes, and line styles. It specifies neither a mandatory Part B palette nor verified source RGB/HEX values.
- Defines the limits: writing guidance does not replace algorithm implementation, experiments, or mathematical proofs, and does not guarantee acceptance. Current submission rules require a separate official check. The package contains original notes and source links, without redistributing reference papers or figures.

### 简体中文

`partb-vrp-writing` 首个版本，面向拟投 *Transportation Research Part B: Methodological* 的车辆路径及相关交通运筹论文。

- 提供“**定位问题 → 判断依据 → 落实修改 → 复查结果**”流程，支持规划、起草、修改与审阅，覆盖论证、文献综述、符号、模型、算法、证明、实验、语言、图与表。
- 包含英文执行指令、六份专题与证据参考文件、Codex 展示信息及空白稿件上下文模板。[English README](README.md) 与[简体中文 README](README.zh-CN.md) 可相互切换；稿件工作使用用户要求的语言。
- 区分学术要求、可调整的写作习惯和作者偏好；保留研究假设与有证据支持的贡献，尊重仅修改表达的要求，并检查受影响的论断、符号、引用及双语对应内容。
- 记录 Su 等（2023）、Sun 等（2018）和 Zamal 等（2025）的部分已核对位置，区分作者稿与发表版、图像检查与仅文本核对；Sun 等的记录仅支持文本层面的观察，不声称逐字审阅整篇论文或全面调查期刊风格。
- 保留作者偏好的**较明亮图表配色**，将白底黑色标签作为可调整的默认选择，按信息需要使用颜色、形状和线型；不规定 Part B 必须使用的色板，也不声称已核实原图的 RGB/HEX 色值。
- 明确边界：写作指导不替代算法实现、实验运行或数学证明，不保证录用；当前投稿规则需另行核对官方要求。包内仅含原创笔记与来源链接，不分发参考论文全文或原图。
